## Three target tasks for FL-02 through FL-04

| Target task | Done well means |
|---|---|
| **Turn technical ML evidence into a public-safe capstone case-study section.** | A 275–350-word section states the problem, validation approach, observed result, and recommendation; every concrete claim traces to supplied evidence; it makes no causal or client-specific claim. |
| **Write an employer-facing summary of one completed ML project.** | Exactly three sentences and 90–120 words; names real tools or methods, states one verified result, and includes no private data or inflated language. |
| **Create and critique a portfolio sitemap for an early-career ML profile.** | Five or fewer primary navigation items, a visible case-study path, one clear contact/action route, and one purpose plus CTA per page; it never claims an unshipped page is live. |
